I am Shuo Xie (解说), a third-year Ph.D. student at Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago, advised by Professor Zhiyuan Li. I am currently interested in optimization and LLMs.
Previously, I received a B.S. degree in statistics from Peking University and a M.S. degree in statistics from University of Chicago and did research in natural language processing with Hongyuan Mei and Hao Tan.
